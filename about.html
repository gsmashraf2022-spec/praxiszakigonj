Your genesis to success.

Praxis Zakiganj is an English language training institute serving students in Zakiganj and across Sylhet — built around exam-realistic practice, small-group attention and continuous support.

Our approach

We train for the exam you'll actually sit.

Most of what worries students about the CD IELTS exam isn't the English — it's the unfamiliar screen. Our computer lab runs the same interface as test day, so by the time a student sits the real exam, the format itself is no longer a source of stress.

Alongside exam preparation, we run a dedicated Spoken English track for students who simply want to communicate confidently in daily life, at work, or with family abroad — no exam required.

Facilities & support

What's included around every course.

  • 01

    Computer-based mock lab

    The same software and layout as the official CD IELTS exam, used for every full and partial mock test.

  • 02

    Praxis Care Unit (PCU)

    Ongoing mentoring and doubt-clearing support for enrolled students, outside of scheduled class time.

  • 03

    English Speakers' Club

    A weekly speaking circle where current students practise together in a low-pressure setting.

  • 04

    Guidance for children and beginners

    A gentler, activity-led entry point for younger learners and complete beginners.

  • 05

    Basic computer & digital skills

    Practical computer literacy and an introduction to freelancing/digital marketing for students who want it.
